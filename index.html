How it is built

Six steps, in the order a child actually learns

Not the order a textbook uses. A child says amma long before they can read it, and reads it long before they can write it — so that is the order here. Each language walks the same ladder.

💬

Speaking

Words first, by the things a child already has names for — family, food, animals, colours. Every word has a voice saying it.

📚

Reading

The same words again, this time to be read rather than said. Nothing new to learn, and the letters start to come apart on their own.

✍

The script

The alphabet, the vowel signs, the stacked letters — and stroke-by-stroke animations showing how each one is written.

💭

Sentences

Putting the words together: I go to school, the cat is under the cot. Every sentence broken into its pieces.

📑

Grammar

The cases, the tenses, the joins — taught with examples quoted from sentences the child has already read, never invented.

🌴

Poems

Real verse by real poets, out of copyright and cited to the page it came from, with every hard word written out underneath.

The part that took longest

Every letter, stroke by stroke

Knowing what a letter looks like is not knowing how to write it. Bhaashe animates all 151 letters of three alphabets — 347 strokes in total — each one drawn in the order and the direction a hand actually moves.

▶️

Watch it drawn

The letter builds itself one stroke at a time, with an arrowhead showing which way the pen is going — then again, slower, as many times as a child wants.

📏

Read from a reference, not guessed

Stroke count, order and direction are facts, and a shape does not reveal them. Every letter was taken from a source that states them, then checked — not inferred from how the glyph looks.

✏️

On the letter’s own card

“How to write it” sits under each letter in the alphabet, so writing is one tap from reading it — not a separate lesson somewhere else.

Why it is built this way

Made to be trusted with a child’s time

🔊

A real voice, or none

Where a word has a recording it is a person saying it, credited by name. Where there is none, the card is simply quiet — nothing is faked with a robot voice.

📜

Every poem cited

Only out-of-copyright verse, with the poet, the book and a link to the scanned page it was checked against. If the age of a text cannot be established, it is not printed.

🏠

Screen time, your call

Optional parental controls behind a PIN only you know, and a plain list of what was practised.

🔒

Private by default

No ads, no third-party tracking, nothing sold. What your child practises stays in their own browser.

✈

Works offline

Add it to the home screen and it opens like an app, with or without a connection.
